# Level 1 · Entry <span class="level-badge">Foundations</span>

Goal: understand what servant leadership actually is (and isn't), build the
inner foundations — self-awareness, listening, empathy, trust — that make it
possible, and practice the basic mechanics of empowering others,
communicating, and giving/receiving feedback. You'll finish by writing your
own personal servant leadership philosophy statement.

By the end of this level you'll be able to explain servant leadership in
your own words, recognize your own emotional patterns and triggers, listen
and respond with real empathy, and hand off ownership of work in a way that
builds people up rather than just offloading tasks — and you'll have a
written philosophy statement that captures how you intend to lead.
